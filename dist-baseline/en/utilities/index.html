Main revenue driver

Utility digitalization built for integration, reliability, and scale.

KUEM helps water and gas utilities modernize metering and infrastructure monitoring without disrupting existing systems.

Common utility pain points we address

  • Delayed fault detection and manual reads
  • Data silos between telemetry, SCADA, and billing
  • Non-revenue water and leakage detection challenges
  • Limited visibility across distributed assets

Smart Water Metering

Remote reads, flow anomaly alerts, and district-level analytics to reduce non-revenue water.

  • LoRaWAN and NB-IoT support
  • Consumption profiles and leak indicators
  • Billing export automation

Smart Gas Metering

Secure gas metering telemetry with alarms for pressure deviations and potential leakage events.

  • NB-IoT optimization for deep indoor coverage
  • Tamper and anomaly event handling
  • Accurate, frequent meter data delivery

Infrastructure Monitoring

Condition and performance visibility for pumping stations, valves, reservoirs, and remote sites.

  • Threshold and trend-based alerting
  • Maintenance workflow triggers
  • Operational dashboards for field teams

Integration-ready architecture

Nexavia connects metering and sensor data into your current ecosystem through open APIs, export adapters, and configurable workflows.

  • SCADA connectivity for operations teams
  • Billing and ERP integration for commercial processes
  • Role-based data access for cross-functional users
