Nexavia

Nexavia platform for digital supervision and field-data management

Nexavia is a modular platform for collecting, processing, visualizing, alarming and integrating data from meters, sensors, devices and external systems.

Key capabilities

  • Data collection from meters, sensors and devices
  • Remote reading, supervision and alarms
  • LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, LTE, M-Bus, wM-Bus, MQTT, Modbus, API
  • Dashboards, charts, tables and maps
  • Alarms for errors, thresholds or outages

What is Nexavia

Nexavia connects field infrastructure with digital supervision workflows: remote reading, monitoring, alarms, reports and integrations with existing business systems. IoT technologies are used where they fit the communication and deployment requirements.

Key capabilities

  • Data collection from meters, sensors and devices
  • Remote reading, supervision and alarms
  • LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, LTE, M-Bus, wM-Bus, MQTT, Modbus, API
  • Dashboards, charts, tables and maps
  • Alarms for errors, thresholds or outages
  • Measurement history and reports
  • Integrations with billing systems, SCADA, ERP, Power BI and other systems
  • Multiple users, organizations and access rights
  • Local, cloud or hybrid deployment

Supported technologies

LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, LTE, M-Bus, wM-Bus, MQTT, Modbus, API and other technologies can be connected according to project requirements.

01

Meters, sensors and devices

Field sources that generate readings, events and status data.

02

LoRaWAN / NB-IoT / LTE / M-Bus / wM-Bus

Connectivity and protocol layer for collecting data from distributed infrastructure.

03

Nexavia

Processing, supervision, alarm and data-management platform.

04

Dashboards, alarms, reports and integrations

Operational views, notifications, exports and links to external systems.

Dashboards, alarms and reports

Nexavia provides dashboards, charts, tables, maps, alarm rules, measurement history and reporting workflows.

External system integrations

Nexavia integrates with billing systems, SCADA, ERP, Power BI and other operational or business systems.

Cloud, local or hybrid deployment

The platform can be deployed in cloud, local or hybrid architectures depending on security, ownership and operational requirements.

Who it is for

Nexavia is intended for utilities, municipalities, infrastructure operators, industry, public services and partners that need reliable field-data management.
