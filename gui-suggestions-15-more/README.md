# KUEM — 15 additional GUI concepts

Fifteen high-fidelity interface directions for KUEM, generated after reviewing the current Astro site and applying the Impeccable and UI/UX Pro Max design guidance.

## Desktop — light

1. **Nordic Signal** — minimal industrial editorial design with routed system lines and vermilion accents.
2. **Alpine Precision** — abstract Slovenian topography merged with a calm system-architecture diagram.
3. **Kinetic Cobalt** — high-energy typographic direction built around delivery momentum.
4. **Editorial Ledger** — enterprise engineering presented with report-like typography and a delivery ledger.
5. **Modular Studio** — bold geometric zones and a clear system-integration demonstration.

## Desktop — dark

6. **Operations Room** — accessible technical dark mode with an illustrative system view.
7. **Carbon Editorial** — premium charcoal editorial world with sculptural architecture.
8. **Night Blueprint** — measured system-stack drawing with engineering notation.
9. **Cinematic Topology** — atmospheric connected-flow storytelling without fake telemetry.
10. **Deep Forest Systems** — dark forest and copper material language with a vertical navigation rail.

## Mobile and focused pages

11. **Mobile Light / Thumb First** — three mobile homepage states with clear one-column reading order.
12. **Mobile Dark / Night Engineering** — high-contrast dark mobile homepage and service states.
13. **Mobile High Contrast / Swiss** — expanded navigation, hero and service screens with strong touch affordances.
14. **Nexavia Product / Dark** — focused product narrative using only verified Nexavia wording.
15. **Architecture Service / Light** — focused service page with an illustrative layered approach.

## Shared design constraints

- Real KUEM service names, partner names and contact details from the repository.
- Nexavia wording limited to: “Connected processes, data, and teams.”
- Visible light/dark theme controls.
- Strong hierarchy, accessible contrast and clear primary actions.
- No fabricated metrics, awards, certifications, dates or client logos.
- No generic SaaS card walls, gratuitous glassmorphism, purple gradients, emoji icons or illegible microcopy.

These are visual proposals rather than production screenshots. AI-rendered marks and fine copy should be replaced with official assets and reviewed before implementation.

Generation mode: built-in image generation, one prompt per concept, followed by a targeted factual-copy correction pass.
