# Prompt set

Every concept used this shared brief:

> Create a polished, shippable, high-fidelity KUEM interface proposal. KUEM is a Slovenian software engineering consultancy. Use only the real services “Software development”, “Architecture & consulting”, and “DevOps & platforms”; product “Nexavia” with the phrase “Connected processes, data, and teams”; verified partner names and repository contact details. Include practical navigation and a visible light/dark control. Maintain accessible contrast and one clear CTA. Do not invent metrics, dates, awards, certifications, product functionality, client logos or contact information. Avoid generic card walls, purple gradients, decorative glass, excessive pills, emoji and unreadable microtext.

## Direction prompts

1. **Nordic Signal:** pale mineral white, fog gray, navy and vermilion; oversized headline “Engineering clarity into complex systems”; abstract signal-routing diagram; continuous service rail.
2. **Alpine Precision:** chalk white, spruce green, icy blue and charcoal; headline “Reliable software. Thoughtfully engineered”; topographic contours becoming system architecture; open service rows.
3. **Kinetic Cobalt:** cobalt, white, black and chartreuse; condensed headline “From complexity to momentum”; crisp diagonal vector paths; oversized service chapters.
4. **Editorial Ledger:** warm ivory, ink, muted blue and rust; serif headline “Software built for the long term”; report grid and idea-to-production ledger.
5. **Modular Studio:** cool white, navy, blue and tomato; headline “We make digital systems work”; few large interlocking zones and connected-system diagram.
6. **Operations Room:** midnight, steel, cyan and signal orange; headline “Systems you can depend on”; diagram labeled “Illustrative system view”; non-quantified proof words.
7. **Carbon Editorial:** charcoal, warm white and cobalt; headline “Built with intent. Ready to evolve”; large serif type and a sculptural architectural ribbon.
8. **Night Blueprint:** dark navy, pale blue linework and lime; headline “Architecture for what comes next”; exploded stack labeled Software, Architecture, DevOps and Nexavia.
9. **Cinematic Topology:** ink blue, ivory, ice blue and amber; headline “Connect the work. Simplify the complex”; flowing topology with no fake telemetry.
10. **Deep Forest Systems:** forest, moss, bone and copper; headline “Engineering that moves business forward”; modular material blocks and Discover—Design—Deliver—Evolve.
11. **Mobile Light:** three large phone-width screens for hero, services/process and Nexavia/contact; current-brand blue and white; thumb-friendly controls and full-width CTAs.
12. **Mobile Dark:** three large phone-width dark screens; headline “Reliable systems. Clearly engineered”; cyan and orange controls; one-column services and contact.
13. **Mobile Swiss:** three high-contrast phone-width screens; open navigation, hero “Built for complexity. Designed for change”, and service/process layout; black, white and orange.
14. **Nexavia Product:** dark focused product page; headline “Connected processes, data, and teams”; abstract canvas labeled “Illustrative platform concept”; conceptual labels Processes, Data and Teams.
15. **Architecture Service:** warm-white technical editorial page; headline “Make the right technical decisions”; layered diagram labeled Context, Architecture, Delivery and Evolution; process bands and related services.
